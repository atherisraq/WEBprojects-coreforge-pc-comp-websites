How a real recommendation would work

  1. Reserve budget for essentials: board, RAM, storage, PSU and case.
  2. Pick a CPU and motherboard that share a socket.
  3. Allocate GPU spend according to the workload.
  4. Choose RAM and storage that meet the workload minimum.
  5. Select a PSU rated above the estimated draw.
  6. Check case, cooler and physical clearances.
  7. Total the cost and flag overages and trade-offs.
  8. Show alternatives and explain each choice.

If a budget cannot support a dedicated GPU, an integrated-graphics build is offered with the performance trade-offs explained.
