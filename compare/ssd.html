Which is better depends on the job

Everyday use

An NVMe SSD for the operating system and applications is the largest feel-of-speed upgrade for most builds.

Bulk storage

A hard drive costs less per terabyte but is much slower for random access. Use it for archives, not for the system drive.

Endurance

Endurance and warranty are listed only when sourced. Confirm both with the retailer.
