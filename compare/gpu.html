Which is better depends on the job

VRAM

The Arc B580 and RTX 4070 SUPER list 12 GB; the RTX 4060 and RX 7600 list 8 GB. More VRAM helps with high-resolution textures and some creative applications.

Power and PSU

The RTX 4060 lists the lowest board power (115 W) and the RTX 4070 SUPER the highest (220 W). Check the exact card's power connector and recommended PSU.

Creator software

Support differs by vendor: CUDA and OptiX for NVIDIA, other paths for AMD and Intel. Check the specific application and version, plus hardware encoder support.

Value

Price per frame cannot be calculated without verified benchmarks. Compare prices only against results at your target resolution and settings.
